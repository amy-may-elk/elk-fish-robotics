Do I need a licence to fly it?

Commercial spraying needs a Remote Pilot Licence and a Part 137 certification for chemical application. We don't run those courses, but we connect you with WA-based CASA-credentialed trainers. Every drone comes with on-day setup, a demo and a walkthrough.

Should I buy, or keep hiring a contractor?

Below about 800 ha a season, hiring is usually the call. Above that, with a recurring spray window, owning tends to pay for itself in a couple of seasons. We'll work it through against your operation.

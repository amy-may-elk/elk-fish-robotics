Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy explains how Elk Fish Robotics Pty Ltd collects, uses and looks after your personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our products and services. We have written it in plain English because we think that is what a privacy policy should be.

Who we are

Elk Fish Robotics Pty Ltd is a Western Australian business based in Fremantle. We are the authorised DJI Agras dealer for Western Australia, selling and servicing agricultural spray drones for broadacre farmers, vineyard and orchard operators, and spray contractors across WA.

When this policy refers to "we", "us" or "our", it means Elk Fish Robotics Pty Ltd.

What information we collect

We only collect information you give us directly. When you fill out an enquiry form on this website, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your business or farm name
  • Your location or region
  • Details about your operation or enquiry

We do not collect sensitive information such as financial details, identification documents or health information.

We also collect basic analytics data about how visitors use our website — things like which pages are viewed and how visitors find us. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How we collect it

Enquiry forms on this website are processed through Netlify Forms, a service provided by Netlify Inc. Form submissions are stored securely and forwarded to our team.

Website analytics are handled through Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager. We also use Google Search Console to understand how our site performs in search results. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to collect anonymised usage data.

What we use your information for

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry — that is it. If you ask us about a drone, we will call or email you back to discuss it. If you book a callback time, we will ring you at that time.

We do not use your information for automated marketing, cold outreach or any purpose unrelated to the enquiry you made. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your enquiry data passes through Netlify's infrastructure as part of form processing, and analytics data is processed by Google as described above. Both are standard, reputable services operating under their own privacy frameworks. Beyond that, your information stays with us.

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or by a regulatory authority, but that is the only exception.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as is reasonably necessary to manage our customer relationships and meet any legal obligations. If you would like your information removed sooner, just ask — see the contact details below.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us what personal information we hold about you and to request that we correct or delete it. To do either of those things, send us an email at the address below and we will get back to you within a reasonable time.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies placed by Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site. These cookies do not identify you personally. Most browsers allow you to control or block cookies through their settings — if you prefer not to be tracked, you can do that without affecting your ability to use the site.

Links to other sites

Our website may contain links to external sites — for example, DJI's product pages. This privacy policy covers our site only. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

Changes to this policy

If we make any significant changes to how we handle your information, we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date at the top. We recommend checking back occasionally if this is something you want to keep an eye on.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of Western Australia, Australia. We comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles where applicable.

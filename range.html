Agras T100

The T100 is DJI's large-capacity Agras, built for operators where spray days are billable days. Big tank, fast coverage, designed for contract and broadacre work at scale.

  • Spray tank: 100 L
  • Spray flow rate: up to 30 L/min (standard 2 nozzles; higher with optional 4-nozzle kit)
  • Spreading: 150 L tank, up to 400 kg/min
  • Lifting payload: 65 kg
  • Safety: Safety System 3.0 with millimetre-wave radar, LiDAR and multi-camera vision
  • Positioning: RTK centimetre-level

In Australia, operated within local regulations at a maximum take-off weight of 149.9 kg.

Agras T25P

Built for operations where the larger machines are more than you need. Vineyards, orchards, hilly country, mixed-use blocks. Compact, foldable, and designed so one person can run it, from loading to take-off.

  • Spraying: 20 kg, flow rate 16 L/min (up to 24 L/min with the optional 4-nozzle kit)
  • Spreading: 25 kg, high-precision screw feeder spreading system 4.0
  • Take-off weight: under 55 kg with a full tank, single-person operation
  • Safety: Safety System 3.0 with millimetre-wave radar and Tri-Vision
  • Transmission: O4
  • Positioning: built-in RTK; optional D-RTK 3 AG for centimetre-level accuracy
